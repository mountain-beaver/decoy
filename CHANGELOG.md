# Changelog

Notable changes to Decoy. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## 0.1.1

- Clearer start. While nothing is shared, a band above the editor says so and has a "Show in Discord" button. The switch in the top bar now reads "Show in Discord" instead of "Hidden" and stands out until you press it.
- Once your status is live, a band confirms it and links to the checklist for when it does not show up in Discord.
- The preview says whether it is only a preview or already on your profile, with a way to stop showing it.
- The quick start guide and its checklist explain the start button; the third step can start showing right away.

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Rich Presence editor: activity kind, two lines of text with links, party size, timer, large and small images, up to two buttons.
- Search in Discord's list of about 25,000 games, or use your own application ID with its uploaded images.
- Live preview of the profile card, the member list and the voice channel row.
- Built-in guide to creating your own application in the Discord Developer Portal.
- Profiles with export and import; switching from the tray.
- Tray icon, start with the computer, start hidden, reconnect when Discord restarts.
- Notice when a newer version is out, with a link to the download page. Nothing installs by itself, and the check can be switched off.
- Windows, macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon) and Linux (deb, rpm, AppImage). On Linux the tray lives in a separate process of about 3 MB.
